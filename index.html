Selected work

Three projects that shipped, scored, and won.

Each one runs end-to-end — from raw data to a decision someone can act on. Two took national titles; one is a live AI agent you can talk to right now.

01🏆 WINNER
FNB DataQuest 2026

Credit Risk Analytics Platform

A regulation-first credit scorecard for retail term loans, built to satisfy Basel IV and South Africa's National Credit Act. Instead of a black-box model, it strengthens logistic regression with natural cubic splines for non-linear age & macro risk, and calibrates the approve/decline cut-off with Youden's J — every parameter transparent enough to explain to an applicant. The dashboard also ships an embedded AI analyst that reads the outputs and interprets them for the user in plain language.

About

I'm a Business Science (Analytics) graduate reading for my MSc in Statistics & Data Science at the University of Cape Town, and a Business Intelligence Engineer at Yoyo Group. My research interests sit at the meeting point of time-series analysis, Bayesian methods and machine learning — and how they hold up in the real world.

Alongside the technical work, I've spent years leading and mentoring: as a sub-warden, House Committee member and tutor in UCT residence. It's shaped how I work — the best analysis is worth little if you can't help people understand and trust it.
